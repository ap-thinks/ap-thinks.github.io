Business Problem

Approve More →
Higher checkout conversion · More merchant revenue · Lower friction for borrowers
⇌
← Approve Fewer
Lower default losses · Better unit economics · Healthier portfolio risk profile
A 5-percentage-point increase in approval rate (70% → 75%) increases completed purchases by approximately 7%, but increases expected credit losses by approximately 18% — highlighting why the approval threshold is a business decision, not just a model hyperparameter.

★ Mid-risk segment is the primary lever for responsible growth. Modest approval rate relaxation here adds meaningful conversion volume with manageable loss impact.

The sensitivity analysis shows portfolio margin peaks at ~78% mid-risk approval rate, after which incremental defaults outpace incremental revenue. The optimal threshold is a function of the business's risk appetite — not the model's default 0.5 cutoff.

funnel_drop_off.sql
-- Stage-by-stage funnel metrics with risk tier cut

WITH funnel_base AS (
    SELECT
        session_id, merchant_id, risk_tier,
        DATE_TRUNC('week', session_date)  AS session_week,
        reached_page_load,
        passed_bnpl_click,
        passed_form_start,
        passed_form_complete,
        passed_approved,
        passed_purchase_complete
    FROM funnel_events
),

overall_funnel AS (
    SELECT
        'Overall'                                       AS segment,
        SUM(reached_page_load)                          AS page_loads,
        SUM(passed_approved)                            AS approvals,
        SUM(passed_purchase_complete)                   AS purchases,
        ROUND(AVG(passed_bnpl_click)         * 100, 1) AS click_rate_pct,
        ROUND(AVG(passed_approved)           * 100, 1) AS approval_rate_pct,
        ROUND(AVG(passed_purchase_complete)  * 100, 1) AS purchase_rate_pct,
        -- End-to-end conversion
        ROUND(
            SUM(passed_purchase_complete) * 1.0
            / NULLIF(SUM(reached_page_load), 0) * 100
        , 1)                                            AS e2e_conversion_pct
    FROM funnel_base
)

SELECT * FROM overall_funnel
UNION ALL
SELECT * FROM tier_funnel
ORDER BY segment;
